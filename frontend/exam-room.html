Identity check and exam recording

Before the exam, you will authenticate a test identity through eSignet and capture a camera photo for session monitoring. During the exam, video and audio will be recorded and automatically checked for identity continuity, additional people, selected prohibited items, head orientation, speech, and recording problems. Page visibility, window focus, and device interruptions are also recorded for review. Observations are saved for human review; AI flags do not disqualify you.   • Your answers save automatically. Wait for the Saved status before navigating.
  • The timer starts when you select Start exam. A confirmed exit before submission disqualifies the attempt. Connection interruptions allow up to 3 minutes of recovery each, with a 10-minute total limit. The timer continues.

Only you may be visible during the exam. Your camera and microphone will be recorded and analysed from Start exam until submission or exit. Automated observations are saved for human review.
