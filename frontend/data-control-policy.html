ASSESSBRIDGE DEMONSTRATION

Data Control

Updated 18 November 2026

Before you start

Read the privacy notice and consent to recording before continuing. Authenticate with the fictional eSignet test identity and capture your session photo. You can retake the photo before sending it. The required checks must pass before the exam starts.

During the exam

Your answers, recording pieces and monitoring observations are linked to your candidate account and exam attempt. Authorized company personnel can review the available evidence. Automated observations do not make review decisions.

Connection loss and exit

The exam timer continues during connection problems. Reconnect to restore access; a restarted session requires recovery checks and a fresh face comparison. If recovery limits are exceeded, the attempt ends as interrupted. An unexplained loss of contact is not proof that you exited. A confirmed exit after Start exam and before submission disqualifies the attempt. After submission, wait for the final upload confirmation before using Exit exam.

Results and evidence

A reviewer records a reasoned decision and any written-question grades. An auditor checks the reports after the exam closes. Candidate results become visible after publication, and published results cannot be changed through the normal workflow. Recordings or missing sections remain identified as incomplete where applicable.

Retention and deletion

The exam notice shows its saved retention periods. Identity photos normally expire 30 days after capture; recordings normally expire 30 days after the attempt ends. Company policy changes apply to new assessments. Case expiry removes the exam content, assignments, answers, results, review findings and evidence. Minimal audit metadata remains until its separate expiry. Case and audit records have no automatic expiry unless a period is configured.

Requests and retained copies

Contact the assessment organizer for data access or deletion requests. There is no self-service account deletion on this site. Live-service cleanup cannot erase exports already downloaded or copies held in backups. Backup copies can retain deleted records until their separate retention ends. The organizer must agree on account, export and backup handling before using the service for real assessments.
