Privacy Notice

AssessBridge uses your registered email, invitation, saved answers, and exam session to deliver your assessment. Before an exam starts, you are asked to capture the photo ID card required by the assessment and a camera image. The server reads the card name and compares its portrait with the camera image. This is not verification against the issuer's records and does not prove liveness. Captured identity images are uploaded only when you select Check identity. The device preview itself is not uploaded.

For a monitored assessment, AssessBridge records webcam video and microphone audio from Start exam until submission, timeout, or exit.

The recording is uploaded in short pieces and stored in encrypted files on the AssessBridge server. A network or device interruption can leave a gap; the system does not label missing footage as recorded. The employer personnel who created the assessment can view available footage through an authenticated page. Access to recordings is logged. Local AI processing checks identity continuity against the verified camera image, additional people, configured phones, coarse head orientation, speech activity, and recording quality. Timestamped observations and recording references are saved for human review. These observations do not automatically disqualify candidates and do not establish misconduct. Sunglasses classification, exact gaze tracking, speaker identification, and liveness authentication are not supported. A copy of the verified camera image is encrypted and held for the monitoring retention period.

Identity images are encrypted in MySQL and linked to your account and invitation. Each check also keeps a record of its result, stage, time, and matching scores without another image copy. The assessment organizer can see whether the latest check passed, failed, or has not been completed; this interface does not display the images. Identity images and recorded media are scheduled for deletion after 30 days in this development setup. Check records may remain after the images are deleted. The employer must approve production retention, storage, access policy, and this notice before real candidate assessments are conducted. Contact the assessment organizer for questions about your data or an exam interruption.
