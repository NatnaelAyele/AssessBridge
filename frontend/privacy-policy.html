ASSESSBRIDGE DEMONSTRATION

Privacy Notice

Updated 18 November 2026

Your account and identity

AssessBridge uses your registered email, name and account details to manage invitations and exam access. eSignet authentication (Fayda national ID simulation) uses a fictional test identity. It does not authenticate a real national ID. A camera photo is captured for session monitoring and recovery; it does not prove liveness.

What is recorded

Webcam video and microphone audio are recorded from Start exam until submission, timeout, interruption or exit. Both are required. Recordings upload in pieces; connection or device problems can leave missing footage.

Monitoring and review

Automated checks can flag a possible identity change, an additional person, configured phones, books, tablets or calculators, head-orientation changes, speech activity and recording problems. Flags support human review and do not establish misconduct or automatically change a score. Exact gaze, speaker identification, sunglasses detection and liveness verification are not supported.

Who can access your information

Authorized personnel and auditors in the assessment company can view the available report, recording and reference photo. Evidence access is logged. Published results are visible to the candidate. Company administrators manage staff access and retention settings.

Storage and retention

Account and exam records are stored on the hosted service. Identity photos and recordings are encrypted at rest; public connections use HTTPS. The service runs on school-provided servers outside Ethiopia. The exam notice shows the retention periods saved for that assessment. The default is 30 days for identity photos and recordings. Case records and audit metadata have no automatic expiry unless a company administrator sets a period.

Copies and questions

Expired evidence is removed from the live service. Case expiry removes exam content, answers, results, review reasons and evidence; limited action types, account references and timestamps can remain until audit expiry. Downloaded exports and encrypted backups have separate lifetimes. Backups are retained for up to seven days, subject to available space; offline backup copies may remain longer until their next successful cleanup. Contact your assessment organizer about access, retention or deletion requests. This service is an educational demonstration; real assessment use requires an agreed privacy and data-handling policy.
