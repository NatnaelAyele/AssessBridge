Privacy Notice

Before an exam, the eSignet simulation authenticates a fictional test identity and shares its name with AssessBridge. A camera photo is captured for session monitoring and recovery. This does not verify a real Fayda identity or prove camera liveness.

For a monitored assessment, AssessBridge records webcam video and microphone audio from Start exam until submission, timeout, or exit.

The recording is uploaded in short pieces and stored in encrypted files on the AssessBridge server. A network or device interruption can leave a gap; the system does not label missing footage as recorded. The employer personnel who created the assessment can view available footage through an authenticated page. Access to recordings is logged. Local AI processing checks identity continuity against the session camera image, additional people, configured phones, coarse head orientation, speech activity, and recording quality. Timestamped observations and recording references are saved for human review. These observations do not automatically disqualify candidates and do not establish misconduct. Sunglasses classification, exact gaze tracking, speaker identification, and liveness authentication are not supported. A copy of the session camera image is encrypted and held for the identity-image retention period.

Identity images are encrypted in MySQL and linked to your account and invitation. Each check also keeps a record of its result, stage, time, and recorded authentication details without another image copy. The assessment organizer can see whether the latest check passed, failed, or has not been completed; this interface does not display the images. The exam notice shows the saved retention periods for identity images, recordings, case records and audit metadata. Images expire after capture; recordings expire after the attempt ends. Case and audit periods run from the assessment closing time. Case expiry removes the assessment content, candidate assignments, results, answers, reasons, notes and evidence. Only action types, account references and timestamps remain until audit expiry. If no period is set for case or audit records, those records do not expire automatically. Downloads and backups are not deleted by platform cleanup. The employer must approve production retention, storage, access policy, and this notice before real candidate assessments are conducted. Contact the assessment organizer for questions about your data or an exam interruption.
